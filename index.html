seedsong

Touch the ground to plant a seed. Every plant is a one-of-a-kind species, and every bloom sings its own notes into the garden’s endless song.

Tap a flower to read its name and its secret. Tap the sky and a seed will ride the wind down. Butterflies carry pollen — seeds that land between two flowers become their child. Stay for nightfall, and hope for rain.
